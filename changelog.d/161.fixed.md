A fresh install runs its first connections full sync within 5 to 20 minutes of the scheduler starting (snapped into active hours), instead of seven days later. Spec 9.4 says the full sync "runs on first setup and weekly", and every later incremental sync compares against it. Only a never-scheduled full sync is affected: a restart restores the stored weekly due time, and the other job kinds keep their one-interval first fire.
