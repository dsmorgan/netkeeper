A fresh install runs its first connections full sync within 5 to 20 minutes of the scheduler starting (snapped into active hours), instead of seven days later. Spec 9.4 says the full sync "runs on first setup and weekly", and every later incremental sync compares against it. Changing the timezone or active hours before that first sync has run keeps it due soon, in the new hours. Once it has run, a restart restores the stored weekly due time and a timing change reschedules it a week out, as before. The other job kinds keep their one-interval first fire.
