The filter builder's field-switch test walks a representative sample of columns instead of every ordered pair. The sample holds every enum and the first two columns of each other kind: 100 switches instead of 484, about 0.7 s instead of 2.8 s. It no longer needs its 20 s timeout. A companion test checks that the sample makes every kind-to-kind and enum-to-enum transition the full walk makes. `NETKEEPER_EXHAUSTIVE=1 pnpm test` still walks every pair.
