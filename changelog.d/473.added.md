`netkeeper linkedin message-check <contact id>` runs a LinkedIn prefill's steps up to the Message click and stops, to find out why a prefill would refuse the click. It opens the contact's profile in its own tab, scrolls briefly, scrolls back up if the top card's Message button is covered, and never clicks, types, or focuses anything. It prints what it found to the terminal only: the viewport, scroll and zoom, each Message link and button with its position, what sits over each click point, and the decision the click would make, with no names, URLs, or page text. It takes the same gates and browser lock as a prefill, counts as one profile visit, and spends no prefill budget.
