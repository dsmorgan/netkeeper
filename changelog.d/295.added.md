`netkeeper serve` now detects replies and bounces to campaign email. Every `[campaigns] reply_poll_minutes` (10), it reads each armed mailbox's Gmail history, or, when that history has expired, the campaign threads and a search for each contact's address. A reply from the contact, in the thread or in a fresh email, ends the enrollment as `replied` before any later step fires, and is stored as an inbound message (subject and snippet, never the body) with an `email_in` interaction and the campaign's label. A delivery-failure notice marks the message, the address and the enrollment `bounced`. A reply saying "unsubscribe", "remove me" or "stop emailing" sets the contact do-not-contact and the enrollment `opted_out`. A follow-up whose thread holds a new message from the other side is not sent. Nothing is deleted in Gmail.
