Contacts API: list with a filter tree, sort, pagination, and column selection (`POST /api/v1/contacts/query`); quick search; one contact with its emails, phones, links, positions, and interleaved timeline; hand edits that stick under field provenance, with a revert to the last synced value; emails, phones, and links; merge; soft archive and unarchive. Bulk actions apply to a filter behind a count confirmation token: ask `POST /api/v1/contacts/bulk/count` for the count, send its signed five-minute token back with the action, and a selection that has moved is refused rather than applied to rows nobody saw.
