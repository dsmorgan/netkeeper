A campaign email whose send got no answer from Gmail is no longer marked failed. netkeeper looks it up in Gmail by its Message-ID and records whether it went out. It is never sent a second time. Ending an enrollment or merging two contacts no longer discards an email that may already be on its way, and a Gmail draft left over from a merge is deleted. A campaign's mailbox can't be changed once the campaign leaves draft, and stopping `netkeeper serve` waits up to 45 seconds for a send in progress to be recorded.
