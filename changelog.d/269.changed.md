A campaign email whose send got no answer from Gmail is no longer marked failed. netkeeper looks it up in Gmail by its Message-ID and records whether it went out. It is never sent a second time, and it is ruled not sent only after several searches over at least two hours, since Gmail's search can lag. A send that Gmail turned away for a reason that may pass (a rate limit, an outage, a mailbox waiting to be reconnected) is tried again 15 minutes later instead of failing the step. Ending an enrollment or merging two contacts no longer discards an email that may already be on its way. A Gmail draft left over from a merge stays in Gmail for you: netkeeper deletes nothing there. A campaign's mailbox can't be changed once the campaign leaves draft, and stopping `netkeeper serve` waits up to 45 seconds for a send in progress to be recorded.
