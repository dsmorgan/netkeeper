`netkeeper campaigns` manages campaigns from the terminal with `create`, `enroll`, `list`, `status`, `activate`, `pause` and `resume`, each with a matching `/campaigns` route; `activate` goes through the review gate only, and when the review is incomplete it lists what is missing and exits non-zero. `netkeeper simulate --campaign ID` replays a campaign's schedule in a throwaway database and shows the sends per day and step, without sending anything or changing the campaign.
