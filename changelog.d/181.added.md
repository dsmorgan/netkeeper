The LinkedIn page can now clear a checkpoint session flag and clear heat by hand, each behind a confirmation that says what it changes. Like `netkeeper linkedin clear-flag`, each clears only what you were shown: a flag or heat raised again while the dialog was open is refused, not cleared. Every manual clear, from the page or the command, logs a warning naming what it cleared. The Browser card also shows the last known state of the LinkedIn session and whether a recent run could not reach Chrome, from what `netkeeper preflight` and runs recorded. The page never checks the browser itself. Below the `sm` breakpoint, the sidebar now opens from a menu button instead of taking up half the screen.
