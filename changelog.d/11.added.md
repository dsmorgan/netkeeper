Identity resolution and merge (`netkeeper.crm.identity`, spec 8.2). Importers and the sync hand each row to `resolve()` as an `IncomingContact` (emails and slugs lowercased, the slug derived from a profile URL, never from a URN) and get back `Matched`, `Candidate`, or `New`: a URN match, then a public-id match (current slug or a recorded alias, which also learns the incoming URN), then any email, then first name, last name, and company together, which is only ever a candidate for review. Two contacts sharing an address, or a slug whose contact carries a different URN, are candidates too. `apply()` creates or updates the contact under the field-level precedence rule, records the source of every field it writes, keeps an old vanity slug as an alias, writes a `contact_snapshot` when a job field changes, and upserts emails, phones, links, and positions by natural key so a re-import never duplicates them. `merge()` folds one contact into another: children move and deduplicate, empty fields fill from the loser with its recorded source, the loser's slug becomes an alias, `met` keeps the more decided value, `do_not_contact` sticks, notes join, and the loser records `merged_into_id`, so it resolves to its survivor from then on.
