Campaigns now pass a review gate before they go active (API only; the screens come with P3-11). A campaign in review needs its 10 sampled previews and any enrollment you looked up approved, a test send of each email step, a clean lint, and its guard summary acknowledged. `POST /campaigns/{id}/activate` refuses with a `409` that lists whatever is missing, and a later change to a step, a template or the audience undoes the approvals it affects. A test send goes only to the campaign mailbox's own address, with a `[Test]` subject, needs the mailbox armed to send, and never counts toward a cap or the recency guard.
