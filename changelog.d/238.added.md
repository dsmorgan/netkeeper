A do-not-send list keeps email addresses no campaign sends to, whichever contact holds them. An address lands on it when a message to it bounces, when its owner asks to unsubscribe, or when you mark it bounced or invalid on a contact. Merging contacts, removing the address, or deleting the contact doesn't take it off; only you do, with `netkeeper do-not-send remove` or **Remove** under **Settings**. The campaign guards check the list at enrollment and at every step and exclude a listed address as `do_not_send`: a bounce or invalid address blocks email steps, and an opt-out blocks every channel. When you merge two contacts that hold the same address, the survivor keeps the worse of the two statuses. Addresses match exactly after trimming and lowercasing, so `name+nk1@gmail.com`, `name+nk2@gmail.com`, and `name@gmail.com` are three different addresses. The `campaign-audience` export skips a listed address and leaves out any contact holding an opted-out one. Because marking an address invalid lists it, the export now leaves out invalid addresses, apart from one whose entry you removed. Migration 0029 fills the list from the bounced and invalid addresses and the opt-outs already recorded. For opt-outs, it lists every address of a contact with any inbound message classified as asking to unsubscribe, even if `do_not_contact` was cleared later, and including addresses the contact gained after the reply.
