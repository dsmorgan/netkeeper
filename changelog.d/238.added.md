A do-not-send list keeps email addresses no campaign sends to, whichever contact holds them. An address lands on it when a message to it bounces, when its owner asks to unsubscribe, or when you mark it bounced or invalid on a contact. Merging contacts, removing the address, or deleting the contact doesn't take it off; only you do, with `netkeeper do-not-send remove` or **Remove** under **Settings**. The campaign guards check the list at enrollment and at every step and exclude a listed address as `do_not_send`: a bounce or invalid address blocks email steps, and an opt-out blocks every channel. When you merge two contacts that hold the same address, the survivor keeps the worse of the two statuses. Addresses match exactly after trimming and lowercasing, so `name+nk1@gmail.com`, `name+nk2@gmail.com`, and `name@gmail.com` are three different addresses.
