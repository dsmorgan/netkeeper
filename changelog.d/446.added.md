You can enroll chosen contacts that the recent-contact guard skipped. On the campaign's Audience step, **Show contacts skipped for recent contact** lists them with when and how someone last contacted them; pick some or all and confirm **Enroll anyway**. The CLI does the same with `netkeeper campaigns enroll ID --override-recent-contact ID,...`, after a prompt. Only the recent-contact guard is set aside, and only for the contacts you name; every other guard still applies. The enrollment records who overrode the guard and when, and the enrollment row shows it. The override sets aside only the last contact you were shown, and a contact someone reached again since you looked is refused, not overridden; contact dated after that, and any contact after a merge of the contact, makes the guard apply again.
