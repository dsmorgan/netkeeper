You change day-to-day settings on the Settings page instead of in `config.toml`: LinkedIn budgets (refused above their hard maximums, with the notes above 100 profile visits and 20 LinkedIn messages a day), active hours and the weekend multiplier, campaign defaults (daily cap, send spacing, the recent-contact guard, the reply poll interval, holidays), the LLM switch and backups. Each value shows where it comes from. `config.toml` is now optional and holds overrides: a key it sets wins and shows on the page locked, with the file's path. Changes apply from the next request, campaign tick or LinkedIn run, and command-line runs obey them too; the reply poll interval and the scheduled-run timetable say when `netkeeper serve` needs a restart. LinkedIn auto-send stays file-only. `netkeeper config adopt` moves an existing config file's values onto the Settings page once, after asking, and keeps a backup of the file.
