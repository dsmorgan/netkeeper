`netkeeper serve` now sends campaign email through Gmail, but only from a mailbox you arm, and every mailbox starts disarmed. Arming is two steps: first for drafts (every step becomes a draft you send yourself), then, once netkeeper has found one of its drafts by its Message-ID, to send. Use `netkeeper gmail arm`, `gmail arm --send` and `gmail disarm`, or the controls in Settings. `gmail status` and the dashboard's mailbox card show the mode.
