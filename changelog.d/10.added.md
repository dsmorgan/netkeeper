Contact models and their migration (`0002`): `contacts` with LinkedIn identity, triage, do-not-contact, removal and enrichment counters, notes, and archive fields, plus the child tables `contact_emails`, `contact_phones`, `contact_links`, `contact_positions`, `contact_snapshots`, `contact_aliases`, and `interactions`, every one user-owned with provenance (`source`, `observed_at`) and a database-level cascade from its contact. Uniqueness is per user (`li_urn`, `li_public_id`, alias slugs, an address per contact); emails and slugs are stored lowercase; `preferred_name` starts as `first_name`. Sessions that may write now start SQLite transactions with `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` (`session_scope(write=True)`, and every request whose method is not `GET`, `HEAD`, or `OPTIONS`), so a read-then-write waits for a concurrent writer instead of failing with "database is locked".
