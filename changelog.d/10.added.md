Contact models and their migration (`0002`): `contacts` with LinkedIn identity, triage, do-not-contact, removal and enrichment counters, notes, and archive fields, plus the child tables `contact_emails`, `contact_phones`, `contact_links`, `contact_positions`, `contact_snapshots`, `contact_aliases`, and `interactions`, every one user-owned with provenance (`source`, `observed_at`) and a database-level cascade from its contact. Uniqueness is per user (`li_urn`, `li_public_id`, alias slugs, an address per contact); emails and slugs are stored lowercase; `preferred_name` starts as `first_name`. Provenance is per field: `contacts.field_sources` records which source last wrote each LinkedIn field, and `netkeeper.crm.provenance` holds the precedence rule (`sync` over `archive` over `csv` over `manual`; `manual` always wins for the fields a person owns). `contacts.last_contacted_at` is a denormalized copy of the newest outbound interaction's time, kept current by whatever writes outbound interactions, so filters and sorts on it never scan the timeline. Sessions that may write now start SQLite transactions with `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` (`session_scope(write=True)`, and every request whose method is not `GET`, `HEAD`, or `OPTIONS`), so a read-then-write waits for a concurrent writer instead of failing with "database is locked".
