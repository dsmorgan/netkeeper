A LinkedIn prefill no longer fails to click **Message** when a copy of the button sits off screen or under a message bubble: it clicks the profile's own Message button, or another one that is on screen and uncovered, and refuses without clicking when none is. A click that still fails records why (for example, covered or off screen) in the run's details. A prefill now also refuses before it clicks when any message bubble is already open in Chrome, minimized ones included, and that includes a leftover bubble for the same contact: close it before you choose Try again.
