The template editor has a merge-field helper: a list of every field a template can use, each with a short description and an example value. Activating a field inserts `{{ field }}` at the cursor in the subject or body, whichever you were in last. The examples are made up until you pick a contact in the preview, and then they're that contact's values. The list comes from the new read-only `GET /api/v1/templates/merge-fields`, which builds it from the names lint allows. Lint now reports the line of each finding (`line` on every lint issue), and the editor shows each finding under its field, at its line, with one sentence on why it matters; the body also marks the lines that have one.
