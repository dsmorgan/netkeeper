The per-test time limit is 10 s instead of 5 s. Under full parallel load a few ordinary tests took 4 to 5 s on a 4-core machine and went over 5 s on a busy 10-core one, so the old limit failed passing tests at random. CI now prints the twenty slowest tests on every run, so the remaining headroom is visible. Three frontend timing tests also get headroom from contended measurements: the filter builder's walk of every field pair has an explicit 20 s timeout, triage by mouse has its own per-contact ceiling (250 ms) instead of sharing the keyboard's 120 ms, and the 10,000-row table's mount and scroll budgets are 2 s and 1 s. The connections-sync tests draw their page waits from a seeded generator, so a cancel test no longer fails on about one run in 250.
