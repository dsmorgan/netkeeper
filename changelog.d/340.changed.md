`netkeeper posture` and the Settings page's posture section now lead with a short summary: one line per protection with its state, and every warning. Run `netkeeper posture --details`, or select **Show details** in Settings, for each row's full detail, its notes, the schedule, and what the report cannot see. The exit code and the API's `ok` are unchanged, and the API adds a `summary` to each protection beside its full `value`.
