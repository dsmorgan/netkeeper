LinkedIn prefills, and every step, email included, for a contact with a LinkedIn conversation, now wait while the LinkedIn inbox poll is stale, the way Gmail follow-ups wait while the Gmail reply poll is behind. The inbox is stale when its newest complete poll started more than two poll intervals and 50 minutes ago (6 hours 50 minutes with the default 3-hour interval), or when none has completed yet. A poll that stopped part way doesn't refresh it. A held step isn't skipped or failed: it goes out after a poll completes, either the scheduled one or one you run with `netkeeper linkedin inbox`, which works while scheduled runs are disarmed. The posture row "linkedin reply poll" and the LinkedIn inbox line in the background-checks popover say why a step is held.
