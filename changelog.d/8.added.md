`scripts/install-launchd.sh` installs a user LaunchAgent (`fun.tnkr.netkeeper`) that runs `netkeeper serve` at login and restarts it after a crash, with logs under `<data_dir>/logs/`. `--data-dir`, `--host`, and `--port` configure it, `--dry-run` shows the plist and the `launchctl` commands without changing anything, and `--uninstall` removes it.
