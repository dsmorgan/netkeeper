The dashboard shows the LinkedIn run that is going, live from the event stream: its kind, status, progress, and the last few contacts it touched, each linked to the contact with what happened. From there you can cancel the run (it asks first), pause an enrichment and resume it later from where it stopped, and pause or unpause the schedule. Pausing the schedule holds new scheduled runs without disarming, survives a restart of `serve`, and replays nothing when you unpause: each kind runs at its next due time. The CLI has the same controls: `netkeeper linkedin pause <run id>` and `netkeeper linkedin schedule pause` and `unpause`, and `schedule status` and `netkeeper posture` say when the schedule is paused.
