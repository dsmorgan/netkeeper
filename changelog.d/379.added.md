A campaign's LinkedIn steps are now ready to prefill instead of skipped: the minute tick lists each due one, and you claim one at a time with `POST /campaigns/linkedin/prefill` or `netkeeper campaigns linkedin prefill`. The claim runs every check an email send runs (the scheduled start, the sending hours, step approval, the guards, never twice) plus one open prefill at a time, LinkedIn's active hours, the session and heat, and a new `li_prefills` budget (10 a day, hard maximum 20). A prefill nobody sent goes stale after three days; you can discard it, or ask for a check that it was sent. A LinkedIn step now needs the contact's LinkedIn member id, which a connections sync adds; enrolling a contact without one raises its enrichment priority. Discarding a prefill counts its step, and the next step is due its delay after the discard. The prefill itself, the browser part, arrives with P4-03: until then a claim that passes every check is refused and records no message or run, though a refusal can still end or park the enrollment as the minute tick would.
