The Settings page now holds the time zone, the warm-up ramp, how long before a profile is enriched again, how many missed syncs mark a contact disconnected (all live), and the Chrome debug address (loopback only, needs a restart; `netkeeper browser launch` and `make chrome` use it at once). A setting that needs a restart says so, and after a save, or when `config.toml` changed on disk, a banner offers **Restart netkeeper**: under the login item `serve` exits and launchd starts it again; otherwise it re-execs itself, and the page reconnects. A restart never interrupts a LinkedIn run, the browser, a background task, a campaign send or the nightly backup: the banner names what is running and offers **Restart when idle**, which you can cancel. Changing the time zone carries today's LinkedIn budget counts and email caps into the new zone, so it never grants extra budget, and the page allows one change an hour.
