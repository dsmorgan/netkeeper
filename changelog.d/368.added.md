The template editor has a **Draft with your AI assistant** helper. Describe the campaign in a short form, choose **Copy prompt**, and paste the prompt into an AI chat assistant you already use; then paste its reply back and choose **Paste result** to fill in the subject and body and lint them at once. The prompt lists the merge fields and lint rules netkeeper checks, uses placeholders like `{{ first_name }}`, and never includes a contact's data. netkeeper makes no AI call and stores no AI key. The new guide, `docs/ai-drafting.md`, covers the prompt, privacy, and how to check the result.
