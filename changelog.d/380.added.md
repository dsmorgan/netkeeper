The LinkedIn inbox poll now reads your messaging page. `netkeeper linkedin inbox` and `netkeeper serve` open `/messaging/` in your Chrome, scroll the conversation list for older conversations, and open at most five conversation pages, only those with a prefilled message or a live enrollment. It reads only what the page loads and sends no request of its own. A page it can't read stops the poll as `route_changed`, and only a poll that completes counts as fresh. The `linkedin reply poll` posture row shows the last complete poll.
