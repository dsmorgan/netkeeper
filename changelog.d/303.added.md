You can add one contact by hand: **Add contact** on the Contacts page, `POST /contacts`, or `netkeeper contacts add`. It takes a first or last name, and optionally an email address, company, title, LinkedIn profile URL, tags, and a static list, and runs the same checks and deduplication as an import. Someone already in your contacts by email or LinkedIn URL is never added twice: you get a link to the existing contact instead (a `409` naming it). A match on name and company alone asks first, and you can add the contact anyway. The new contact's source is `manual`, and the auto-tag rules run over it.
