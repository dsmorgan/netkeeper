Scheduled connections runs now stop after three runs in a row of one kind (full or incremental) end `answer_lost`. A lost answer moves neither heat nor the route-changed breaker, so before this change a page whose answers kept arriving unreadable would cost page views on every scheduled fire. Each kind keeps its own count, separate from the route-changed breaker, so a daily incremental that completes does not hide a weekly full sync that keeps losing answers. The counts show in `netkeeper linkedin schedule status` and in the posture report as the "answer-lost limit". A run that completes with nothing lost clears its own kind's count, and `netkeeper linkedin schedule reset-breaker` now clears every count. Manual runs are never refused.
