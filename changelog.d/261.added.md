Campaign guards now look past the contact to the address: a contact is excluded when its sendable address is already on another enrollment in the same campaign (`duplicate_address`), or is bounced or invalid on another of your contacts (`address_bounced_elsewhere`, email steps only). Both reasons appear in the excluded summary.

The campaign engine (spec 11.3, 11.4): `netkeeper serve` runs a minute tick that fires due campaign steps one at a time, inside each campaign's send window and away from holidays, under the per-mailbox and per-campaign daily caps, spaced by a human-like delay, and never twice for the same step. The next step is timed from when the previous message actually went out. An email step waits while its mailbox needs re-authorization. Nothing is sent yet: sending and drafting arrive with P3-07.
