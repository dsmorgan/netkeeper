Per-day and per-week budgets and a heat score for the LinkedIn extractor (spec 9.6, 9.7), keyed by LinkedIn account (`account_id`, a plain integer until P2-06 adds the `linkedin_account` row) and, for the day/week boundary, by the account owner's local timezone rather than UTC. `netkeeper.services.budgets.consume` checks and records one unit of `connection_pages`, `profile_visits`, `inbox_polls`, or `li_messages_auto` atomically, clamping a configured default to spec 9.6's hard max and refusing only once a period's count already exceeds its limit — a run can end at most one unit over budget, never two. `netkeeper.linkedin.heat` is the pure, unit-tested decay math (a raised score decays exponentially, computed fresh on every read rather than ticked down by a job) behind a cooldown multiplier, a skip threshold, and a per-run budget shrink that never reaches zero; `netkeeper.services.heat` persists it per account in `settings_kv` and adds the manual clear.
