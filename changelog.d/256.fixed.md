`netkeeper gmail login --timeout` is now a hard bound: a connection that opens and sends nothing, such as a browser preconnect, no longer holds the wait past it.
A Google answer cut off part way (an `IncompleteRead`) is treated as Google being unavailable: the Settings callback says so and `POST /mailboxes/{id}/check` answers the mailbox unchanged, instead of both answering 500.
A mailbox is marked `reauth_required` only when Google says the grant or client is dead (`invalid_grant`, `invalid_client` or `unauthorized_client`), never on a 5xx whatever its body says. A proxy's `407`, a `403` or `404` with an HTML body, `invalid_request`, or an answer without an access token used to pause email steps; now the next poll tries again.
