The Enrollments, Contacts, and Inbox lists say when a search or filter narrows them: "Showing 12 of 87 · filtered by:", each search term or filter as a chip you remove with a click or the keyboard, and **Clear**. A filter that matches nothing says so, with **Clear filters**, instead of the empty-list message. `POST /api/v1/contacts/query` takes `count_unfiltered` and then answers `unfiltered_total`, the count without the filter's predicate.
