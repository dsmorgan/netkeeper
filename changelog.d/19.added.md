Interactions and notes (`netkeeper.crm.interactions`, spec 8.1 and 10.1): `POST`, `PATCH`, and `DELETE` on a contact's interactions, a paged `GET /contacts/{id}/interactions`, `GET /contacts/{id}/timeline` interleaving interactions and job snapshots newest first behind a `before` cursor, and `PUT /contacts/{id}/notes` for Markdown notes. Outbound kinds (`email_out`, `li_out`, `call`, `meeting`) keep `contacts.last_contacted_at` at the newest outbound time; editing or deleting one recomputes it from the rows that remain, and `recompute_last_contacted()` repairs the column for every contact in one statement. Another user's contact answers `404`. The isolation harness now runs list endpoints whose path carries a parameter, formatting it per user through `ListEndpoint.path_params`.
