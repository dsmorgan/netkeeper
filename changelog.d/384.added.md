LinkedIn auto-send, off by default (ADR 0008). With `[campaigns] linkedin_auto_send = true` and a step in `auto_send` mode, `netkeeper serve` sends that step's messages itself: during LinkedIn active hours, 20 to 45 minutes apart, within the `li_messages_auto` budget, paused while heat is at its skip threshold, after checking again that the message box is the contact's and holds exactly the message. The queue, the campaign page and Waiting for you show which steps auto-send. After a message goes, netkeeper closes its bubble and its own tab; anything else leaves the bubble for you and holds auto-send until you close it and resume. `netkeeper serve` reads the setting at start. A migration adds `messages.send_clicked_at`.
