Campaign email can now be sent or drafted through Gmail (spec 11.5). A `send` step goes out at once. A `draft` step becomes a Gmail draft for you to send, and once you send it, the next step is timed from when it went out. Follow-ups reply in the first email's conversation with a `Re:` subject, and every campaign message gets the campaign's Gmail label. A draft you delete instead of sending ends that contact's sequence. `netkeeper serve` doesn't send with it yet.
