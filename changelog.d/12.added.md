Import a LinkedIn data archive (`netkeeper.crm.archive`, spec 9.2 and 10.5). Point it at the export zip, at a directory it was unpacked into, or at one of its CSVs, and `Connections.csv` becomes contacts with provenance `archive`, while `messages.csv` and `Invitations.csv` become `li_in` and `li_out` interactions for the timeline and for triage evidence. Tables are recognized by their header rather than their file name, so the preamble that opens `Connections.csv` is skipped however long it is and however much LinkedIn changes it, and an export with no preamble reads the same way. An email address is written only for the few rows that carry one. Most message rows name no profile URL, so conversations are grouped by their id and attributed whole from any row inside them that does; group threads and conversations nobody signed are reported rather than guessed at, and so are dates in no recognizable form. Re-importing the same archive adds nothing, and a field edited by hand keeps the edit while the archive's own value stays available to revert to.
