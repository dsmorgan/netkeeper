You can now set `profile_visits_per_day` up to 250 a day; the hard maximum was 100. The default stays at 60. Above 100 a day, netkeeper warns that the higher rate makes it more likely that LinkedIn restricts your account or asks you to verify it, but it does not stop you or ask you to confirm. The warning appears in `serve`'s startup log, as a note in `netkeeper posture` and on the Settings page (a note leaves the report clear and the exit code 0), in `GET /linkedin/budget` (`risk_warning`) and on the LinkedIn page's budget panel, and when you arm scheduled runs from the CLI or the UI. The weekly limit now follows the daily one: leave `profile_visits_per_week` out of `config.toml` and it is 5 × the daily limit (300 at 60 a day), up to 1,250. A `profile_visits_per_week` you set still applies, clamped to 1,250. If your `config.toml` sets `profile_visits_per_week`, for example the old default of 300, delete that line to get the derived value; `netkeeper posture` adds a note when the value you set is below 5 × your daily limit. A daily limit you raise applies at once on an install already past its warm-up.
