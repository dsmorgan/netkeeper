Templates name contact fields only: the `me.*` fields and the `[me]` config section are gone (#320, #342). A template that uses `me.*` gets a `removed_field` lint error and can't be activated until you edit it. Your own details now live on the self contact, which you edit on the Settings page under About you. A test send renders the template with those details and goes only to the campaign mailbox's own address. An old config with `[me]` still loads, with a deprecation warning; its name, website and city seed the self contact once. The self contact never appears in your contacts, search, triage, exports, lists, merges, duplicate hints, campaigns, enrichment, LinkedIn sync matching or counts. Removing `me` from the step fingerprint makes every review record made before this change stale once, so approve, test and lint each reviewing campaign again.

If an active or paused campaign has a step template that uses `me.*`, that step sends nothing until you publish a new template version without the field. The campaign page says why on each blocked enrollment, the posture report warns, and netkeeper logs a warning when it starts.
