Your own details now live on the self contact, which you edit on the Settings page under About you. A test send renders the template with those details and goes only to the campaign mailbox's own address. An old config with `[me]` still loads, with a deprecation warning; its name, website and city seed the self contact once. The self contact never appears in your contacts, search, triage, exports, lists, merges, duplicate hints, campaigns, enrichment, LinkedIn sync matching or counts.
