Profile enrichment is stricter about whose answer it reads: a redirect counts only when it comes from the profile asked for or an earlier redirect of the same visit, and a throttle or wall on a renamed profile now stops the run even when the page never finished loading. A streamed copy cut mid-stream is no longer read as whole. Scheduled runs never come back minutes apart: a scheduled job that runs more than five minutes late (after a sleep, a restart, or a late re-offer) has its next run a full interval after it actually ran, and a scheduled full sync gets at most one re-offer per week even across a restart or an unreachable browser.
