Connections sync: a full or incremental pass over your LinkedIn connections list creates and updates contacts, writes a snapshot when a headline changes, and marks a connection disconnected only after two complete full syncs in a row miss them. A full sync that stops early for any reason (budget, throttle, checkpoint, a changed page), or that would mark an implausible share of your network at once, never marks anyone, and no sync starts after a checkpoint until you clear the session flag. Not yet scheduled or runnable from the command line: it waits on the browser-side fetch (#150).
