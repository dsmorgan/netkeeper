The Campaigns page is live. You can build a campaign from your templates (name, mailbox, and ordered steps, each with its delay, mode, condition and same-thread setting), take its audience from a list or a filter, and enroll that audience through the guards, which show the excluded summary with its reasons. The review gate is one screen that lists what is still missing. From it you approve sampled and searched previews for the fingerprint they were shown with, run lint, send a test of each email step to your own mailbox (which must be armed for send), and acknowledge the guard summary. Activation asks for confirmation first and, when the gate refuses, lists what is missing. A campaign's page also shows each step's fired and sent counts and its enrollments, and lets you pause and resume it. `GET /campaigns/{id}/enrollments` lists and searches a campaign's enrollments, each step in a campaign's answer now carries its `id`, and the campaign list includes each active campaign's next send. Creating a campaign on a mailbox that needs reauthorizing or is disabled is refused with a 409.
