The connections sync now moves the pointer to rest over the connections list before it scrolls. Playwright's `mouse.wheel` fires at the virtual pointer's position, which starts at `(0, 0)` and sits under LinkedIn's fixed global nav rather than the list, which scrolls inside its own container: a wheel replay that never moved the pointer scrolled nothing at all, so the first supervised sync's tab never scrolled. `BrowserRun.scroll` now rests the pointer over the page's content once per tab, with a few short, jittered, paced `mouse.move` hops, before its first wheel event. The rest point is read from the page's own on-screen content box, not guessed at a fraction of the tab's viewport size, since that guess missed the connections list's own centered column on a wide monitor and had nowhere reliable to land on a narrow one.
