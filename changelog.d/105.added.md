Added `netkeeper.services.scheduler`: APScheduler-backed jobs for connections full sync, connections incremental sync, enrichment, and inbox poll (spec 9.4), scheduled by name against an injected `JobRegistry` — a trivial no-op until P2-06 and P2-07 register real handlers. Next-fire times persist in `settings_kv` per `(account_id, kind)` so a restart resumes the schedule instead of restarting it; a fire missed while nothing was polling — a restart, an outage, or a laptop that slept with the process still running — catches up exactly once, 5 to 20 minutes after the first poll that notices, never once per missed interval; a settings write that doesn't change a job's timing (interval, timezone, or active hours) leaves its due time untouched instead of rebuilding it; and above the configured heat threshold (spec 9.7) a browser job is skipped without disturbing the cadence, on by default rather than on request. Any two job kinds sharing an account stay at least two minutes apart (spec 9.5's "never run enrichment and a message send in the same minute"): `stagger_due_times` spreads the due times a schedule is established with, and a poll that has already fired one kind defers every other kind that was due to a later poll, so at most one runs per account per poll. `netkeeper.services.simulate` replays the schedule against a virtual clock — no sleeping, no real APScheduler loop — for exercising restarts, downtime, and collisions in milliseconds.
