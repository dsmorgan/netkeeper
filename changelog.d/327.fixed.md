The posture report's `scheduled jobs` row no longer reads `unknown` on every install that runs `netkeeper serve`. It names each job kind as scheduled, missing, or not applicable, and says why the LinkedIn inbox poll is not applicable (it has no runner yet). A new `reply poll` row shows when each armed mailbox, armed for drafts or to send, was last polled for Gmail replies. It adds a note, not a warning, when a mailbox has never been polled or when its last poll is more than three intervals old: follow-ups that start a new conversation are held until replies are checked again. Arming scheduled LinkedIn runs now gives a served job kind that has no due time its first one, without waiting for `serve` to restart, once `serve` has established the schedule, and never moves a due time already set.
