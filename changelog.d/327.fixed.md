The posture report's `scheduled jobs` row no longer reads `unknown` on every install that runs `netkeeper serve`. It names each job kind as scheduled, missing, or not applicable, and says why the LinkedIn inbox poll is not applicable (it has no runner yet). A new `reply poll` row shows when each armed mailbox, armed for drafts or to send, was last polled for Gmail replies, and warns when the poll is more than three intervals late or has never run. Arming scheduled LinkedIn runs now gives a served job kind that has no due time its first one, without waiting for `serve` to restart, and never moves a due time already set.
