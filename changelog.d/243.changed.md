The template editor no longer throws away an unsaved draft without asking. Opening another template, starting a new one, or leaving the Templates page asks you to confirm first, and closing or reloading the tab gets the browser's "leave site?" prompt. The guard is a shared `UnsavedChangesGuard` component that other editors can reuse.

While the template editor waits to lint the text you just typed, it dims the previous lint result and says "Checking…", instead of showing the old result as if it were current.
