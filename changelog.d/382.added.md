LinkedIn steps can now be prefilled. When you click **Prefill**, netkeeper brings its Chrome tab to the front, opens the contact's profile, clicks **Message** once, and types the message with human timing into the one composer it verified is empty, focused, and addressed to that contact. It never presses Enter and never clicks Send: you read the message and send it yourself. Newlines are typed as Shift+Enter, so multi-line LinkedIn templates now lint clean. A prefill that can't verify the composer types nothing and gives the step back; one stopped partway is never retyped. Each prefill spends one `li_prefills` and one `profile_visits` unit before it opens the profile, and a claim that waits more than 60 seconds to start lapses.
