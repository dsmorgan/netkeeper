Settings → Gmail has a setup guide that walks you from no Cloud project to a connected mailbox one step at a time, with links to the exact console page for your project, copy buttons for every value, the `gcloud` commands for creating the project and enabling the Gmail API, and live checks for the client, the token, and the Gmail API. The guide and `docs/gmail-setup.md` now keep the app in Testing with you as a test user, and explain the home page and privacy policy URLs that publishing needs.
