Connect the Gmail account campaigns send from, from the Settings page or with `netkeeper gmail login`, using your own Google Cloud OAuth client. [docs/gmail-setup.md](docs/gmail-setup.md) walks through the setup. The refresh token and the client are stored in the Keychain, never in the database or a log. While `netkeeper serve` runs, it refreshes the token every `reply_poll_minutes`. When Google stops accepting it (access revoked, or seven days old on a consent screen still in Testing), the mailbox is marked "needs re-authorizing", email steps pause, and a banner on every page offers to authorize again. `netkeeper gmail status`, `check` and `disconnect` do the same from the terminal.
