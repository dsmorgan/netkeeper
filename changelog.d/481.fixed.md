The prefill no longer refuses a contact's **New message** bubble when its card links the contact more than once, for example by photo and by name, links them by member id instead of their profile URL's name, or links a page under their profile. Any link in the bubble to anyone else, on any element and in any of LinkedIn's profile URL forms, still refuses, and so does a card with no link to the contact. Each case has its own reason. The log says how many links the card had and which form each was in, without any URL or name, whether the card passed or not. Auto-send's Send click uses the same check.
