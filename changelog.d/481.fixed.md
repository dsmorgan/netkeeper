The prefill no longer refuses a contact's **New message** bubble when its card links the contact more than once, for example by photo and by name, or links them by member id instead of their profile URL's name. Every profile link in the bubble must still be the contact's: a link to anyone else, or no link at all, still refuses, each with its own reason, and the log line now says how many links the card had and which form each was in, without any URL or name. Auto-send's Send click uses the same check.
