The review gate now asks for one approval per campaign step instead of one per sampled message. On the campaign page, each step shows one rendered message at a time, with previous and next buttons, the ← and → keys, and a counter such as "3 of 10", so you can page through every enrolled contact; **Approve step** then approves the whole step. The approval covers the step's messages rendered later too, for contacts enrolled or edited since, until you edit the step or its template, which undoes it. A message that fails to render, renders with a lint error or no subject, or whose contact a guard excludes is listed apart and stays blocked: no approval covers it and it never sends. A step whose template uses `{{ personal_line }}` keeps per-message review, and the screen says so. Activation needs every step approved (`step_approvals`), and every message of a `personal_line` step (`message_approvals`). The API adds `GET /campaigns/{id}/review/steps/{step_id}`, `POST .../review/steps/{step_id}/approve` and `POST .../review/steps/{step_id}/messages/approve`, and drops `POST .../review/sample`, `.../review/previews` and `.../review/approve`. The CLI adds `netkeeper campaigns review-step` and `campaigns approve-step`. Migration 0032 adds `campaign_step_approvals`; a campaign already in review needs its steps approved again.
