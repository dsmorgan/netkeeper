The LinkedIn message budgets are higher. `li_prefills` now defaults to 15 a day (was 10) and `li_messages_auto` stays at 15; both have a hard maximum of 50 a day (was 20 and 30). Setting either above 20 a day is allowed, and netkeeper warns about it: `netkeeper serve` logs it at startup, and `netkeeper posture` and the Settings page list it as a note on that budget. Each budget stays separate.
