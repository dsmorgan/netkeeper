Activating a campaign now takes a scheduled start, by default the next Tuesday at 09:00 in your time zone, or "Now" in one click. netkeeper sends nothing for the campaign before its start, and you can change the start until the first send. The hard send window is gone: `[campaigns] send_window_days` and `send_window_hours` are no longer read, and a config that still has them loads with one deprecation warning. netkeeper instead suggests send slots (Tuesday to Thursday, 09:00 to 16:30) and warns, without blocking, when you pick a start outside them. A follow-up aims for the next suggested slot after its delay, unless you give its step a time of day of its own; holidays only steer the suggestion. A batch the daily caps hold back continues the next day at the same time, never overnight. The daily caps, spacing, do-not-contact and every other guard still apply. Posture and the campaign page show the next scheduled send, and remind you that netkeeper sends only while `netkeeper serve` runs and the Mac is awake. Migration 0031 adds `campaigns.starts_at` (set to the activation time on existing campaigns) and `campaign_steps.send_time`.
