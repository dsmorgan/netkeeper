Activating a campaign now takes a scheduled start, by default the next Tuesday at 09:00 in your time zone, or "Now" in one click. netkeeper sends nothing for the campaign before its start, and you can change the start until the first send. The hard send window is gone: `[campaigns] send_window_days` and `send_window_hours` are no longer read, and a config that still has them loads with one deprecation warning. netkeeper instead suggests send slots (Tuesday to Thursday, 09:00 to 16:30) and warns, without blocking, when you pick a start outside them. A follow-up aims for the next suggested slot after its delay, unless you give its step a time of day of its own; holidays only steer the suggestion. New sending hours, a global setting on the Settings page (or `netkeeper campaigns sending-hours`), say when campaign email may go out; the default is Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00, and "any time" turns them off. Only a campaign's start ignores them: its first batch goes at the start you choose, whatever the hour, until the daily caps stop it that day. The rest of the batch resumes at the next opening of the sending hours and uses that whole day; follow-ups, explicit step times, retries, and anything left over while the Mac slept wait for the sending hours too. Campaigns already active before this release have no chosen start, so all of their sends keep the sending hours. If the stored sending hours ever cannot be read, nothing sends, and the Settings page shows the defaults ready to save. A step that keeps failing to send now gives up after 16 tries rather than after a day. The daily caps, spacing, do-not-contact and every other guard still apply. Posture shows the sending hours and when the next send will really go, the campaign page shows the next scheduled send, and remind you that netkeeper sends only while `netkeeper serve` runs and the Mac is awake. Migration 0031 adds `campaigns.starts_at` (set to the activation time on existing campaigns) and `campaign_steps.send_time`.
