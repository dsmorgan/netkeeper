A full connections sync that reaches a natural end a little short of LinkedIn's own stated total now still completes and ages contacts, instead of aging nobody every week. LinkedIn's total can count people the list itself never serves (a deactivated account, for instance); a full sync is now allowed a small slack below it (the larger of 5 and one percent of the total), and a run that used it says so in its notes and `counts_json` ("complete with 4 short of LinkedIn's total"). A shortfall past the slack keeps the previous behavior, and the end-of-list rule for a short final page is unchanged.
