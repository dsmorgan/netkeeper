The Contacts table no longer shows a wider selection than a saved-view link names. A `?view=` link waits for the saved views to load before it queries, says so if they fail to load or the view no longer exists, and warns when the view filters on more than the filter bar can show (changing a filter control would widen it). Next, Previous, and a new filter or sort now open at the top of the table. A page past the last one lands on the last page, and `POST /api/v1/contacts/query` refuses an `offset` above 1,000,000,000 with a 422 instead of failing with a 500. Picking rows one by one stops at 1,000, the most a bulk action takes, and says to select the whole filter instead. A request the API's schema refuses now shows the reason instead of a bare "422". On a contact's page, every editor shows a write to a merged contact as a link to the contact it was merged into, and the Met and Do-not-contact editors now show a failed write at all. The detail page's unreachable "merged into" banner is gone.
