Template lint now checks LinkedIn messages for what LinkedIn and the prefill accept, using the same rules the prefill types by. A subject, a body over 8,000 characters, a line break, or a character the prefill can't type (a tab or other control character, an invisible formatting character, a stray tag sequence) is an error, so a campaign with such a step can't be activated. Emoji sequences, such as a family emoji or the flag of England, are fine. A body over 1,000 characters, or a template expected to take over four minutes to type (about 1,100 characters of prose), is a warning, and the template list shows warnings in their own badge. A rendered message gets the same checks, and there a message expected to take over four minutes to type is an error, so step review blocks a message whose merge value adds a line break or too much length, and you can't approve it. Email templates are unchanged.
