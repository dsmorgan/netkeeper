Template lint now checks LinkedIn messages for what LinkedIn and the prefill accept. A subject, a body over 8,000 characters, a line break, or a control character such as a tab is an error, so a campaign with such a step can't be activated. A body over 1,000 characters is a warning, because the prefill takes minutes to type it, and the template list shows warnings in their own badge. A rendered message gets the same checks, so step review blocks a message whose merge value adds a line break or length, and you can't approve it. Email templates are unchanged.
