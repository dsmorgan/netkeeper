Template lint now checks LinkedIn messages for what LinkedIn and the prefill accept. A subject, a body over 8,000 characters, or a line break in the body is an error, so a campaign with such a step can't be activated. A body over 1,000 characters is a warning, because the prefill takes minutes to type it. A rendered message gets the same length and line-break checks, so step approval also catches a merge value that adds either. Email templates are unchanged.
