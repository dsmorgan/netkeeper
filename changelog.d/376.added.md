`netkeeper.linkedin.pacing.typing_plan`: a pure, seeded plan for typing a LinkedIn message one character at a time, the way a person does. Delays are lognormal around 140 ms a character, with extra pauses at word and sentence ends, an occasional thinking pause, and a 40 ms floor. A line break is its own Shift+Enter step, never a bare Enter, and a multi-line body stays refused until Shift+Enter is shown never to send. A body whose plan runs past 300 seconds raises `TypingTooLong` before any key, and `typing_length_warning` flags a body of 1,000 characters or more. Nothing types it yet: the LinkedIn prefill (#382) replays the plan.
