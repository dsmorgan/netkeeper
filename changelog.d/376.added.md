`netkeeper.linkedin.pacing.typing_plan`: a pure, seeded plan for typing a LinkedIn message one character (grapheme cluster) at a time, the way a person does. Delays are lognormal around 140 ms a character, with extra pauses at word and sentence ends, an occasional thinking pause, and a 40 ms floor. A newline (CR, LF, or CRLF) is its own Shift+Enter step, never a bare Enter, and a multi-line body stays refused until Shift+Enter is shown never to send. Every other line break, and control, bidi, zero-width, private-use, unassigned, and invisible filler characters are refused, as are tag sequences other than the England, Scotland, and Wales flags, variation selectors other than U+FE0E and U+FE0F, and more than one of those in a character. A body whose plan runs past 300 seconds raises `TypingTooLong` before any key. For the template lint, `typing_expected_seconds` estimates a body's typing time against a 240-second threshold, `typing_length_warning` flags a body over 1,000 characters, and `is_untypable`, `is_untypable_cluster`, `LINE_BREAK_CHARS`, and `NEWLINE_CHARS` give the character rules.
