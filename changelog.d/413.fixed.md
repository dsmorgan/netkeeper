A Gmail mailbox that needs signing in again no longer makes netkeeper read your other mailboxes every minute. Each healthy mailbox is polled for replies once every `[campaigns] reply_poll_minutes`, as before. The mailbox that needs signing in is skipped, keeps holding its follow-ups that start a new conversation, and is polled at the next minute after you sign in again. A mailbox whose poll stopped part way is also polled again on its own.
