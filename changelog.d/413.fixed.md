A Gmail mailbox that needs signing in again no longer makes netkeeper read your other mailboxes every minute. Each healthy mailbox is polled for replies once every `[campaigns] reply_poll_minutes`, as before. The mailbox that needs signing in is skipped and polled at the next minute after you sign in again. Until that poll reads its inbox, its follow-ups that start a new conversation stay held, even if its last good poll was recent. A mailbox whose poll keeps failing is retried after 1, 2, 4 and more minutes, up to the poll interval, instead of every minute. A mailbox netkeeper can't open because the Keychain is locked now says so in the poll status.
