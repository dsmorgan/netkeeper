The core of the LinkedIn inbox poll. A poll reads the conversations active since the last complete poll and records each message with a contact you already have as an `li_in` or `li_out` timeline entry, with a snippet of at most 200 characters. It matches people by their LinkedIn URN only, never creates a contact, and skips group threads. A repeat poll writes nothing twice, and an archive import after a poll does not record a polled message again. Each poll spends one `inbox_polls` budget unit before it reads, and follows the heat, session-flag, and active-hours rules of the other runs. Start one by hand with `netkeeper linkedin inbox` or `POST /linkedin/runs` with `kind: inbox`. The page source that reads the inbox arrives with P4-01, so until then a poll ends failed with that reason, and `netkeeper serve` does not schedule it. Migration 0035 adds the `li_conversations` table and `interactions.external_id`.
