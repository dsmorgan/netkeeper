The UI runs a campaign's LinkedIn steps. The dashboard and each campaign's page show the LinkedIn queue: due steps, each with a **Prefill** button (and **Prefill next** on the dashboard). Nothing prefills until you click, and only one prefill is open at a time. While it types, the queue says "Typing in Chrome…" with the run's live progress; a refused prefill says why and that nothing was typed. **Waiting for you** lists prefilled messages to send yourself in Chrome, with their age, **I sent it, check now**, and **Discard**; stale ones are highlighted. A LinkedIn step shows its ready, prefilled, sent, and stale counts, and the step builder shows each template's lint findings and offers auto-send only when `[campaigns] linkedin_auto_send` is on, with its risk. The inbox badges each item by channel and links a LinkedIn reply to its conversation. The LinkedIn page names inbox polls and prefills in its runs list and adds **Check inbox now**. Settings shows "manual linkedin sends" first, highlighted as a warning when auto-send is on, and an **Acknowledge** button clears the first inbox poll's warning (`POST /linkedin/inbox/acknowledge`).
