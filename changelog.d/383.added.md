The UI runs a campaign's LinkedIn steps. The dashboard and each campaign's page show the LinkedIn queue: due steps, each with a **Prefill** button (and **Prefill next** on the dashboard). Nothing prefills until you click, and only one prefill is open at a time. While it types, the queue says "Typing in Chrome…" with the run's live progress; a refused prefill says why in plain words and that nothing was typed. **Waiting for you** lists prefilled messages to send yourself in Chrome, with their age, **I sent it, check now**, and **Discard**; stale ones are highlighted. A LinkedIn step shows its ready, prefilled, sent, and stale counts, and the step builder shows each template's lint findings and offers auto-send only when `[campaigns] linkedin_auto_send` is on, with its risk. The inbox badges each item by channel and links a LinkedIn reply to its conversation. The LinkedIn page names inbox polls and prefills in its runs list and adds **Check inbox now**. Settings shows "manual linkedin sends" first, highlighted as a warning when auto-send is on, and an **Acknowledge** button clears the first inbox poll's warning (`POST /linkedin/inbox/acknowledge`). Before you click Prefill, and while it types, the UI tells you the recipient may see "typing…" and asks you not to type or click in Chrome. A refusal that follows the Message click tells you to close the empty message bubble; a prefill that stopped part of the way says part of the message may be in the composer. The queue shows the hold "until the inbox is read", and says to run `netkeeper linkedin inbox` by hand before the first poll. A prefill that stopped part way through typing stays in **Waiting for you** as a red row ("Don't click Send"), holds the one-prefill slot, and clears with **I cleared it, discard**. A run records whether it clicked Message (`message_click_attempted`, `message_clicked` in its counts), so the UI knows whether a message bubble may be open.
