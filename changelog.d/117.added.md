A `user_positions` table holds the user's own job history: filled from the LinkedIn archive's `Positions.csv` and editable by hand through `GET/POST /api/v1/me/positions` and `PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/me/positions/{position_id}`. The triage card now carries `worked_together`, the genuine you-and-them overlap this makes possible — same company, overlapping dates, reported with the years the overlap is actually known to span — matching company names loosely across punctuation and legal-entity suffixes and excluding a same-company match that is provably not simultaneous. The address-book `shared_companies` count (#84) keeps its exact name and behavior; the two are never to be confused (#117, #84).
