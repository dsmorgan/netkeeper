The Inbox page is live. It lists the replies, unsubscribe requests and bounces your campaigns detected, newest first, each with its contact, campaign, enrollment status, subject and snippet. You can mark each one handled or unhandled, add a note to the contact from it, and filter by kind or show handled items too. A replied or bounced enrollment on a campaign's page links to its messages in the inbox. `GET /inbox` and `PUT /inbox/{id}/handled` are new, and migration 0026 adds the handled flag.
