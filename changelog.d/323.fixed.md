A contact's first enrichment no longer counts as a job change. A change now counts only when a later enrichment finds a different title or company than an earlier enrichment recorded; a position from the connections list, the LinkedIn archive, a CSV, or your own edit is never the earlier position. The dashboard's **Changed jobs** card and the CRM filter **changed jobs within** now select the same contacts. The filter used to match any snapshot, including a new headline or location alone, so saved filters and smart lists that use it can match fewer contacts. Migration 0030 clears the flag on snapshots stored by first enrichments, imports, and edits; its downgrade leaves the corrected flags in place.
