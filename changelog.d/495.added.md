`netkeeper linkedin message-check <contact id> --bubble` reads the message bubble you opened by hand for a contact, in the tab it is in, and reports how its close control's name relates to the name in its header, in counts and fixed words only. It opens no profile, spends no profile visit, and never clicks, types, or focuses anything. When auto-send leaves a sent bubble open because the close control didn't match, the run's note now says the same shape after the refusal.
