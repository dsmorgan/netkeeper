A manual edit of a LinkedIn field now sticks (CP1, #28): `manual` is the highest rank in `netkeeper.crm.provenance`, so no later sync, archive, or CSV import overwrites a field a person edited, or cleared; among the automated sources `sync` still beats `archive` beats `csv`. Every observation of a provenance field by an automated source is recorded in the new `contacts.synced_values` column (migration `0003`; field to value, source, and `observed_at`) whether or not it reached the live column, so the last synced value is always at hand. `revert_to_synced(contact, field)` puts that value and its source back, `overridden_fields(contact)` lists the edits that hide a different synced value, and a merge fills the survivor's missing synced values from the loser.
