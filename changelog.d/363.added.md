You can merge two contacts from the contact page: **Merge with…** finds the other contact by name or email, previews side by side what survives under the merge rules, lets you swap which contact survives, and asks you to confirm with a summary of what moves. A merge can't be undone. A contact waiting for review now shows "Possible duplicate of …" with a merge action, on its page and on the triage screen, when another contact has the same name, ranked higher when it also shares a personal email address or phone number and lower when its LinkedIn id differs.
