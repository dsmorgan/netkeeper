The campaign tables: campaigns, their steps, enrollments, and messages. Nothing creates campaigns yet. A message can never be deleted by deleting the contact, campaign, step, or template it names, so the record of what was sent survives. Once a campaign that uses a template leaves draft, editing the template saves a new version. A template that any campaign names, including a draft, can't be deleted. The campaign guards decide who may be enrolled and who may be sent each step. A contact is excluded if they are merged, archived, waiting for review, marked do-not-contact, disconnected, have no usable address on the step's channel (a bounced or invalid email doesn't count), are in another running campaign, or were contacted in the campaign's recency window. Each exclusion carries its reason, and the review summary ("212 in audience, 37 excluded: 30 no email, ...") is generated from those reasons. Nothing runs the guards until the campaign engine arrives.
