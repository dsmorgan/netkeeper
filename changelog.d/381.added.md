A LinkedIn reply now ends a campaign sequence, whatever channel its steps use. When the inbox poll finds a message from a contact dated after the first message a campaign sent them, on any channel, the enrollment ends `replied` and no later step fires; a reply containing "unsubscribe", "remove me", "stop emailing", or the new "stop messaging" opts the contact out instead, as an email reply does. A completed enrollment's reply is recorded for 30 days after its last send, and the enrollment stays `completed`. Group threads never count. An answer the poll saw before it knew the message was sent (a Gmail draft the drafts poll sees sent later, or a prefill a later poll confirms) still ends the sequence once the send is known. The poll also confirms LinkedIn prefills you sent: the first message you send the contact after a prefill, edited or not, marks that `prefilled` or `stale` message `sent`, and the next step is timed from it. `netkeeper simulate --campaign` now replays LinkedIn steps as well, one prefill at a time.
