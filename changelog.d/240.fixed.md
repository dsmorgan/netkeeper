Saving or previewing a tag rule whose pattern has a large alternation of words under a repeat is fast again: 165 two-letter branches under `+` took about 8 seconds to check after the alternation guard arrived, and now take a few hundredths of a second. An alternation under a repeat whose branches start with character classes, such as eighty branches like `[xy]z`, used to take up to about 20 seconds to check; past 30 such comparisons the rule is now refused as possibly slow, in well under a second. Other patterns are accepted or refused as before.
