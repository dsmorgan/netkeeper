The campaign page now shows a campaign's results: tiles for sent, reply rate, bounced and opted out, each opening the enrollment table filtered by that status, and a bar chart of the sends per day in your time zone, by week for a long campaign. The steps table adds Replied, Bounced and Opted out columns. A reply or opt-out counts against the last step sent before it, so a reply that arrives while the next step waits to send counts against the step before. A bounce counts against the step of the message that bounced. `GET /campaigns/{id}/results` answers the same numbers, and `netkeeper campaigns status` prints them, with `--json` for exactly what the endpoint answers. A step's sent count now includes a message that bounced after it was sent.
