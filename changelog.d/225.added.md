Message templates, served under `/api/v1/templates`, for email and LinkedIn. A template is written in sandboxed Jinja and can use the merge fields from the spec: the contact's fields, `me.*` from `[me]` in the config, the campaign fields, `personal_line`, and the `ago` filter (`{{ previous_send_date | ago }}` gives "last week"). Every save runs lint, which reports undefined variables, a body with no per-contact field, an email with no subject, and links that do not parse. Lint errors don't block a save, but they will block activation. `GET /api/v1/templates/{id}/preview?contact_id=` renders a template for one contact. Where the contact is missing data, the field comes out empty and the preview adds a warning. Editing a template that a campaign uses saves a new version and leaves the old one alone. Nothing uses templates until campaigns arrive. Templates use a small, fixed part of the template language: merge fields, `if`, comparisons, a few text filters, `~` to join text, and arithmetic on whole numbers. Anything else is refused when you save, so a template cannot make a render run long or produce more than 100,000 characters. A contact's missing data never breaks a render, and a merge value cannot add a line to an email subject.
