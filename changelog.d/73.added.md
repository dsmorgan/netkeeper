Filters can ask who is in a list. `list_member` compiles now: a static list becomes a subquery over its membership rows, and a smart list's own filter is folded into the one you are writing, so a list can be built out of another list. Membership means exactly what the list page shows — an archived or merged-away contact is not a member, and a list belonging to someone else, or one that has since been deleted, matches nobody rather than breaking the filter. Two lists cannot be made to define each other: the save that would close the circle is refused.
