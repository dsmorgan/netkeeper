`docs/implementation-guide.md` gains P2-15, importing the outreach history from a previous mailing tool so netkeeper knows who was already contacted before it sends anything. `.gitignore` refuses `*.xlsx`, `*.zip`, and the LinkedIn archive's file names, because real exports are personal data and belong outside the repository.

`netkeeper history import <workbook.xlsx>` records the old mailing tool's campaigns and the people its workbook names, with an imported **email out** on each matched contact's timeline, so the recency guard counts them. `netkeeper history scan-gmail` then searches Gmail, read-only, for what those people sent back: a bounce or an unsubscribe request goes on the do-not-send list (an unsubscribe also sets **Do not contact**), and any other reply marks the contact **Needs review** until you confirm it. It also searches for replies with each campaign's exact subject, from people the workbook doesn't list, and marks them for review only. Both commands are dry runs unless `--apply`. See [docs/history-import.md](docs/history-import.md).
