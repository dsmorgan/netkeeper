A draft you send with Gmail's Schedule send no longer ends its contact's sequence. Before discarding a draft that left Gmail's Drafts, the drafts poll searches Scheduled for it by its Message-ID. Found there, the message stays drafted until Gmail delivers it, and the next step counts from when the poll saw it go out. A Schedule send you cancel, which puts the draft back in Drafts under a new id, is followed too. A Gmail error in either search changes nothing.
